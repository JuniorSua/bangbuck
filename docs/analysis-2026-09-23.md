## What changes in the conclusion

- **Opus 5.5 is only token-hungry at max.** At max it uses ~119k output tokens per AA task
  ($5.98). At high it uses ~36k ($1.82) and scores 53.6 — above every Astra effort (max 52.7) at
  roughly Astra high's cost ($1.73). At medium it scores 51.2 for $1.34 vs Astra medium's 49.6 for
  $1.54. On AA's suite, Opus 5.5 at medium/high matches or beats Astra per dollar. Whether that holds
  on real repo tasks is the DeepSWE run to wait for.
- **GPT-6 Sol is the everyday model to watch.** In Codex, AA measured it at 69.0% on DeepSWE v1.1
  for $2.99 per task (Astra max in Codex: 67.6% for $7.47). It now also has a WebDev rating (#4).
  Once DeepSWE publishes it, it could challenge Astra low for everyday.
- **Coding in an agent: Fable 5.1 and Astra are level** (62.2 vs 61.6); Astra costs 40% less per task.
